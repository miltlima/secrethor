Secrethor audits Secrets and never modifies them. To audit every namespace:

  $ kubectl apply -f - <<EOF
  apiVersion: secrets.secrethor.dev/v1alpha1
  kind: SecretPolicy
  metadata:
    name: hygiene
  spec:
    allowedNamespaces: ["*"]
    maxAgeDays: 90
    detectUnused: true
  EOF

Then read the results:

  $ kubectl get secretpolicies
  $ kubectl get secretpolicy hygiene -o jsonpath='{range .status.findings[*]}{.reason}{"\t"}{.namespace}/{.name}{"\t"}{.message}{"\n"}{end}'
